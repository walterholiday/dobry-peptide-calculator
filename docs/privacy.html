PepCalc Reconstitution

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. PepCalc Reconstitution does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

How the app works

All calculations run entirely on your device. The values you enter are kept only in the app's memory while it is open and are discarded when you close it. The app makes no network requests.

Children

Because no data is collected, no data is collected from children either.
