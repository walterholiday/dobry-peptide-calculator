**Promotional text** (170 chars max)

**Description**
Peptide Calculator is a simple reconstitution calculator for laboratory use.

Enter three numbers and get the answers you need:

- Vial amount (mg)
- Desired amount per draw (mg)
- Bacteriostatic water added (mL)

The app instantly shows:

- Concentration (mg/mL)
- Draw volume (mL)
- Equivalent units on a U-100 insulin syringe
- Approximate number of draws per vial
- A syringe illustration showing the fill level

Everything runs on the device. No account, no ads, no tracking, no network connection required.

This calculator is for educational and entertainment purposes only. It is not medical advice. Research peptides are intended for laboratory research and in-vitro use only and are not approved for human or animal consumption. Consult a licensed professional before making any decisions related to health or research protocols.
